# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for anyone (human or AI) writing in this repository.

## Writing style

The `README.md`, code comments, and docstrings here use a dry, concrete, technical voice. Match it. In particular, steer clear of the patterns that make text read as machine-generated:

- No marketing adjectives or praise. Don't call things "robust", "powerful", "seamless", "elegant", "comprehensive", or "state-of-the-art". Say what the code does and stop.
- Go easy on emphasis. Don't bold-face words to highlight them and don't sprinkle italics. Introduce a term once, plainly, rather than flagging it.
- Use a precise technical term when it is the right one (L-BFGS-B, differential evolution, MCMC), but don't pile up jargon or invent in-group shorthand. Write "region of interest", not "the band".
- Cut filler such as "Note that", "It's worth noting", "Importantly", "In order to", "leverage", and "utilize". Keep sentences short; don't chain several clauses with em-dashes and parentheticals into one dense line.
- Skip the reflexive "not just X, but Y" and "X isn't merely Y" contrast.
- State caveats and limitations plainly instead of softening them with upbeat framing.

When in doubt, write the way a careful colleague would in a code comment: brief, specific, and a little dry.
